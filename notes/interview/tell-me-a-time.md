# Tell Me a Time

These questions are often used by employers to find out how you deal with difficult situations as well as how you communicate.

## Q: tell me about a time you missed a deadline and how you handled it?

- You want to give a scenario that makes you look good.

- You missed the deadline because of x, you communicated to the stakeholders by saying y, but ensured it wouldn't happen again by implementing z

## Q: tell me a time you had a large positive experience on the business that was not asked of you (you found a problem and fixed it yourself)?

- The requests-cache one gets destroyed with 'why didn't you have the cache in the header', so maybe don't use that one.


## Q: tell me about the most difficult problem you had to solve?

- you want to think through the most difficult scenario you have ever been through

## Q: How do you explain technical concepts to stakeholders or colleagues without a technical background?

- Tetris for tech debt
- Use flow diagrams for showing how the services will communicate with one another
